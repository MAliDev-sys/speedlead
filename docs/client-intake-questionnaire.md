# SpeedLead — Business Info & FAQ Questionnaire

**What this is for:** the answers below get pasted into your SpeedLead
dashboard (Settings → Integrations → Auto-response → "Business info for
the AI"). This is what powers your AI auto-reply — it only ever uses facts
you give it here, so the more detail you provide, the more useful and
specific its replies to your customers will be. It's also what lets it
work out a rough price estimate for a customer on the spot instead of just
saying "someone will get back to you."

Fill in what applies to your business — skip anything that doesn't. Short,
plain answers are fine; you don't need to write full sentences.

## 2. Pricing
This is the most valuable section — give real numbers where you can, even
rough ones. Your AI will only ever present these as *ballpark estimates*,
never a firm quote, and will always say a real quote follows an on-site
look.

- **How do you price jobs?** (per square foot, per linear foot, flat rate
  by job type, hourly rate, etc.)
- **Typical rate(s):** e.g. "Asphalt shingle roof repair: $8–$14/sq ft.
  Metal or tile: $14–$22/sq ft. Full tear-off and replacement: quoted
  after inspection only."
- **What changes the price?** (material choice, job complexity, site
  access, permits, age/condition of existing work, etc.)
